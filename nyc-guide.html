New York City Guide

Some of my favorite spots for food, coffee, shopping, and walks! · Last updated August 8, 2026

Breakfast & Brunch

Dimes Deli

I stumbled upon Dimes one morning in search of a cappuccino. I stayed for 30 minutes, chatting with the bartender and the other patrons. Strong positive energy, and real food.

Apollo Bagels

Open-faced bagels. I recommend the lox or tomato. They will always, always have a line, because they are just that good.

Elbow Bread

Close to Dimes, this place does pastries and coffee the right way! Fluffy, flavorful, and inviting. Seating is limited; go on the weekend for their rotating specials.

Lunch & Dinner

Kiki

Some of the best Greek food I've had and surely the best octopus I've ever had. It's loud if you sit inside.

The French Diner

No reservations, ~10 seats. Go early and put your name down. Three staff are working: the chef, the dishwasher, and a woman who does all else. Everything is divine.

Café Habana

I had the best cubano sandwich I've ever had, and people rave about the street corn (I don't like street corn). Great location for lunch.

Torrisi

Relatively impossible to get a reservation, but you can go by yourself or try to walk in. Known as one of the top-rated Italian restaurants in NYC.

Los Tacos No. 1

Classic, cheap(ish), delicious. Get the pork tacos.

Cho Dang Gol

Korean food, no reservations. Known to be some of the best Korean food in the city. Please get the Spicy Galbi Jjim, one of the best foods I have ever tried.

Chez Fifi

On the Upper East Side, Chez Fifi has beautiful service, a velvety atmosphere, and truly curated meals. Incredible steak tartare.

Golden Diner

If you want to go for breakfast, it will be a two to three hour wait. So go for dinner! Who doesn't want a honey butter pancake at 9pm?! (Best pancake in the world, not my words.)

Quique Crudo

I haven't been, but if you have the chance, go! This Mexican restaurant is highly rated, and almost everyone rates it a 10/10. (Please go and know I'll be jealous.)

L'Industrie Pizzeria

The burrata is my favorite, but you truly cannot go wrong with any option. Be warned: a full pizza will be around $37 (ridiculous!).

Gramercy Tavern

A beautiful place for lunch. A NYC staple, Gramercy's dishes are complemented by fresh vegetables. I highly recommend it.

Wayan

I love French food and I love Asian fusion. The regular menu is great, but you cannot miss getting cheese on your chocolate chip cookie for dessert.

Rooftops & Bars

The Mandarin Oriental

Arguably one of the most critically acclaimed bars in the city. Expensive (and maybe a bit stuffy), but the views of Central Park are unparalleled.

Saint Tuesday

Maybe the opposite of the Mandarin Oriental, this bar is underground in an old ribbon production facility. Live jazz on Friday nights after ~10pm!

Bar Belly

One of the top cocktail bars in the city! Such a fun vibe on Orchard Street in the L.E.S.

Bar Revival

Terracotta walls, great drinks, great energy.

From My Friends

Candidly, I am not the biggest bar-goer, but my friends are, so here are their recommendations (with my descriptions).

Mezzrow

In the West Village, Mezzrow is an intimate piano bar with great cocktails. A perfect spot for post-dinner drinks.

The Nines

Another piano bar, this time in NoHo. Red velvet and cheetah print decor. Maybe a bit performative, but well suited for a fancier pregame.

Chez Zou

A (low, 4th floor) rooftop bar in Chelsea. I imagine it is more corporate, but still pretty views and nice drinks.

Dessert

Mimi's

The BEST frozen yogurt (tart, coffee, mango and more), and even better toppings. Cookie dough, peaches, mochi, boba, chocolate, coconut, I can go on. The line will be 30 minutes.

Grace Street Coffee & Desserts

Korean desserts. Sweet, delish, everything you want.

Coffee

Stone St Café

I love this place because of the art and atmosphere! I kept coming back to sit and do work. Also a great location in SoHo.

Le Gaz

Also on the L.E.S. This place is both a clothing store and a café. Known for their Vietnamese coffee and sweet cream cold foam. I liked it, but loved the name and interior even more.

Kinship

Close to the office! Great coffee (though expensive).

Cafe Paradiso

The most viral coffee in NYC to date.

Walks & Neighborhoods

SoHo / 5th Ave

5th Ave is the classic shopping street, ranging from designer to everyday brands. I recommend starting north, then walking 5th Ave down to SoHo if you want to shop.

Orchard Street

The most classic L.E.S. strip. I walked this street almost every day. Vintage shops and wine bars line the street, and it's closed to cars Thursday to Sunday nights.

West Side Highway

A common walking and biking path along the Hudson River. Walk far enough south and you'll see the Statue of Liberty (amongst the haze).

The High Line

In all honesty, I never did this walk. However, it's known for its great art installations, and it's built on a former rail line winding through the west side of the city.

Seaport

Close to our office, with a cute boardwalk! It feels clean and fresh, though a bit like a movie set.

Bonus: Yoga Studios

Fun fact about me: I love yoga! Here are my favorite studios I tried while in NYC.

Broome St Yoga

The practice and studio draw from Eastern influences. Opening and inviting.

Yoga Spa NYC Midtown

Knowledgeable, calming instructors. Will push you in a good way.

Bhakti Center

Unmatched energy. Truly down-to-earth, in-touch people.
